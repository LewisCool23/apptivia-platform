Section 02

The Problem

Sales teams are drowning in tools but starving for insight. The average B2B sales organization uses 10+ tools, yet managers still coach by gut and reps still waste the majority of their time on non-revenue activities.

Three Failure Modes

Failure Mode 1: Invisible Performance
Reps don't know where they stand until it's too late. Quarterly reviews surface problems 90 days after they start. Weekly KPIs — when they exist — live in spreadsheets that nobody opens. By the time a manager sees the trend, the rep is already on a PIP or has quit.

Failure Mode 2: Gut-Based Coaching
Managers coach based on intuition, not data. They spend their limited coaching time on whoever is loudest, not whoever needs it most. High performers get ignored; struggling reps get generic advice. The $15B spent on sales training annually has no feedback loop to measure impact.

Failure Mode 3: Cold Outreach in a Warm World
Reps blast generic messages to cold lists while intent signals go unnoticed. Companies showing buying behavior — hiring, funding, competitor engagement — aren't prioritized because the data is scattered across 5+ tools that don't talk to each other.

Apptivia is a unified platform that gives sales teams real-time visibility, data-driven coaching, signal-based prospecting, and built-in motivation. Each layer feeds the next, creating a closed-loop performance system that gets smarter with every week of data.

How It Works (5-Step Flywheel)

  1. Connect (10 min): Org admin configures 5 key KPIs via guided onboarding. Apptivia ingests data from CRM, phone system, and calendar via integration framework (10 connectors built: Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Gong, SalesLoft, Marketo, Apollo, Google Calendar, Microsoft Calendar, Sendoso).
  2. Score (Continuous): Every rep gets a real-time performance score updated weekly. KPI Watchdog auto-detects anomalies (rolling 4-week averages) and positive trends (≥15% improvement). Managers see team-wide patterns at a glance.
  3. Coach (Automated): Aaron AI identifies at-risk reps and applies framework-specific coaching using 14 proprietary frameworks. Coaching nudges auto-fire weekly when Tier 1 KPIs fall below 80% for 2+ consecutive weeks. AI generates targeted coaching plans from live KPI data vs. goals.
  4. Compete (Daily): Contests and wallboards create healthy competition. 8 auto-rotating wallboard slides with real-time Supabase subscriptions. Achievements reward sustained excellence via daily auto-award cron (2,213 types awarded on first run, 357 badges).
  5. Prospect (Signal-Driven): Engage surfaces companies showing buying signals from 45+ signal types. AI drafts personalized outreach and queues it for human approval. Pipeline Operator tracks deals through configurable CEP stages with AI forecast notes.

Multi-Tenant Security

Org-Scoping Audit Complete (April 2026): Every INSERT operation across all 20 pages and 98 components has been verified for organization_id inclusion. RLS policies enforce row-level isolation. Tables without their own org_id column (contest_participants, profile_badges, profile_achievements) use FK-based RLS joins through parent tables. 3 missing org_id inserts identified and fixed in the coaching plans flow.

  • Row-Level Security: Every table has RLS policies scoping queries to the authenticated user's organization_id
  • Profile-Based Isolation: All API endpoints run through loadProfile middleware that validates org membership
  • FK-Scoped Tables: Child tables (contest_participants, profile_badges, etc.) inherit org isolation through foreign key joins to org-scoped parent tables
  • Role-Based Access: 4 roles (Admin, Manager, Coach, Power User) with granular permission flags per feature
  • Leadership Exclusion: Admins and managers automatically excluded from scorecard/KPI views to prevent data contamination

Theme 1: Every Role Needs a Different Experience

SDRs, AEs, Managers, and Admins have different daily tasks and KPIs. Apptivia makes role assignment a first-class concept: when tagged "SDR," the Scorecard shows SDR KPIs (Meetings Booked, Response Time), Coach nudges focus on prospecting discipline, and Aaron loads SDR-relevant presets. When tagged "AE," everything shifts to Win Rate, Deal Size, discovery prep.

Theme 2: Signal Intelligence Is the Moat

The 45-signal library with 3-tier taxonomy (Fit → Relevance → Intent) is the most important structural differentiator. Competitors either have no signals (Ambition, Spinify) or only basic intent data (Apollo). Apptivia's signal processing pipeline (Apollo + Tavily + SEC + Claude) creates compound intelligence that improves with every scan.

Theme 3: Unified Data Is Worth More Than Fancy AI

Apptivia's positioning: NOT an outbound tool (vs. Apollo, Outreach), NOT a conversation intelligence tool (vs. Gong, Chorus). It is performance intelligence that sits above all of them, pulling data from wherever it lives, scoring it, coaching from it, and triggering action based on it.

Theme 4: Evolution from Rep Scoring to Org Intelligence

Individual scorecards are table stakes. The next evolution: organizational performance intelligence via the Org Health Scorecard — scoring the organization across 5 dimensions (Team Performance, Pipeline Health, Config Completeness, Engagement Trend, Outbound Motion) with actionable "fix it" links on every Yellow/Red item.

Key Risk: Single Developer

Mitigation strategy: The codebase is not a prototype — it's production-grade with 135 database migrations, comprehensive RLS policies, and well-structured component architecture. Claude Code accelerates development 5-10x. First engineering hire should be a senior fullstack developer who can own features end-to-end. Target: hire at $100K ARR or after seed funding.

What Could Kill Us

  • Gong adds coaching + gamification + prospecting: Unlikely in <2 years. Their DNA is conversation intelligence, not performance management. Acquisition risk is higher than build risk.
  • CRM vendors add native scoring: Salesforce/HubSpot could build basic scoring. But they won't build AI coaching, signal processing, or gamification. They'll be integration partners, not competitors.
  • AI commoditization: Generic AI coaching becomes free. Apptivia's moat is the data (cross-org benchmarks, historical coaching plans), not the AI model itself.
  • Market timing: Sales teams not yet ready for "productized RevOps." Mitigated by starting with simple scorecard (proven need) and expanding.
