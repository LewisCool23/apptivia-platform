Section 02

The Problem

Sales teams are drowning in tools but starving for insight. The average sales organization uses 10+ tools, yet managers still coach by gut and reps still waste the majority of their time on non-revenue activities.

Three Failure Modes

Failure Mode 1: Invisible Performance
Reps don't know where they stand until it's too late. Quarterly reviews surface problems 90 days after they start. Weekly KPIs — when they exist — live in spreadsheets that nobody opens.

Failure Mode 2: Gut-Based Coaching
Managers coach based on intuition, not data. They spend their limited coaching time on whoever is loudest, not whoever needs it most. High performers get ignored; struggling reps get generic advice.

Failure Mode 3: Cold Outreach in a Warm World
Reps blast generic messages to cold lists while intent signals go unnoticed. Companies showing buying behavior (hiring, funding, competitor engagement) aren't prioritized because the data is scattered across tools.

Section 03

The Solution

Apptivia is a unified performance intelligence platform that gives sales teams real-time visibility, data-driven coaching, signal-based prospecting, and built-in motivation — all in one place.

The Performance Intelligence Stack

Apptivia Engage BUILT Find the right prospects with signal-based intelligence
Apptivia Scorecard BUILT Measure what matters with real-time KPI tracking
Apptivia Coach + Aaron AI BUILT Improve rep performance with data-driven coaching
Wallboard + Contests BUILT Drive daily motivation through visibility and competition
Achievements + Badges BUILT Build long-term engagement through progression

Each layer feeds the next. Engage identifies high-intent prospects. Scorecard measures rep activity and outcomes. Coach uses scorecard data to deliver targeted coaching. Wallboard and Contests drive daily motivation. Achievements create long-term engagement loops that reduce rep turnover.

How It Works

  1. Connect: Org admin configures 5 key KPIs in 10 minutes. Apptivia ingests activity data from existing CRM/phone system via integration framework.
  2. Score: Every rep gets a real-time performance score updated weekly. Managers see team-wide trends at a glance. KPI Watchdog auto-detects anomalies.
  3. Coach: Aaron AI identifies at-risk reps and suggests specific coaching interventions based on KPI patterns. Coaching nudges fire automatically when Tier 1 KPIs fall below 80% for 2+ consecutive weeks.
  4. Compete: Contests and wallboards create healthy competition. Achievements reward sustained excellence. Badges auto-award daily.
  5. Prospect: Engage surfaces companies showing buying signals and drafts personalized outreach for rep approval.

Data Architecture Highlights

  • KPI Metric History: Point-in-time config tracking via trigger-based kpi_metric_history table — enables accurate historical scoring even when KPI goals change
  • Single getMonday(): Canonical week-boundary calculation used everywhere (eliminates date drift bugs)
  • Single calcPct(): Unified percentage calculation replacing 13+ scattered implementations, with direction-aware logic ("higher is better" vs "lower is better")
  • In-flight guards: Ref-based dedup prevents double-submission across hooks (useCoachData, useContests, useSignalProspecting)
  • Supabase lock manager: Custom in-memory mutex replaces Navigator LockManager to prevent 10s timeout cascade under high concurrency
  • Notification system: 29 notification types with dedupe keys, priority levels, read/dismiss tracking, auto-cleanup

Section 10

Strategic Pillars

Five principles that guide every product, marketing, and business decision:

Pillar 1: "5 Great KPIs > 50 Messy Metrics"
Apptivia's onboarding guides orgs to pick 5 KPIs that matter, not dump 50 fields. This is both a product constraint (5 KPI slots enforced in ConfigureModal) and a marketing message. Simplicity is the feature.

Pillar 2: "Earn the Right to Automate"
Maturity gating prevents orgs from enabling AI features before they have clean data. The ladder: Dashboard → Alerts → Autopilot → AI Coach → Autonomous. The 10 cron jobs represent the Autopilot stage. Full autonomous mode (Phase 4) requires validated data patterns.

Pillar 3: "Rep-First, Not Surveillance"
Every feature must help the rep before it helps the manager. Achievements = rep-facing. Scorecard = shared. Coach = manager-facing but rep-beneficial. Aaron presets differ by role. We build tools reps want to use, not tools they resent.

Pillar 4: "Cross-Org Benchmarking = Data Moat"
The one thing LLMs can't replicate: real performance data across hundreds of sales orgs. "How does your team compare?" is the long-term defensibility play. Every new org makes the platform more valuable for all orgs.

Pillar 5: "Aaron = Senior Strategist, Not AI Assistant"
Aaron should feel like talking to a VP Sales who's seen 1,000 reps. 14 coaching frameworks cataloged. Sales DNA methodology injection on every prompt. Anti-AI-slop filter via content safety + profanity detection. Human approval on every AI-drafted action in the queue.
